Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bağış Batırer ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Talevia mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency.

1. Who We Are

Talevia is a language learning application developed and operated by Bağış Batırer, an individual developer. If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at support@talevia.app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and username.
  • Language preferences: Your native language, the language(s) you are learning, and your proficiency level.
  • Learning content: Words you save, your quiz results, reading progress, and vocabulary lists.
  • Profile information: Optional display name and profile photo.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: App sessions, features used, screens visited, and time spent in the app.
  • Learning analytics: Game scores, streak data, badge achievements, and vocabulary mastery levels.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, app version, and language settings.
  • Crash reports: Error logs to help us identify and fix technical issues.

2.3 Voice and audio (optional features)

When you use the AI conversation feature, your voice input is processed in real-time. We do not store audio recordings. Voice data is transmitted to our AI partners solely for generating a conversational response and is not retained.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only with trusted service providers who help us operate the App:

  • Firebase (Google): User authentication and analytics. Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: Backend infrastructure and content delivery. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and in-app purchases. Privacy Policy
  • AI providers (Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek): Powering vocabulary translations and AI conversation features. These providers process data under their respective privacy policies and do not use your data to train their models.

We may also disclose your information if required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), in which case we will notify you.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance purposes.

Anonymized and aggregated data (which cannot identify you) may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of analytics tracking at any time in app settings.
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for optional data processing (e.g., notifications).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@talevia.app. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Talevia is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. International Data Transfers

Talevia is a global application. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States and the European Union. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses where required.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Cookies and Tracking

The Talevia mobile app does not use browser cookies. We use Firebase Analytics and crash reporting SDKs, which may collect device identifiers for analytics purposes. You can opt out of analytics collection in the App's settings.

11. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, by sending you an in-app notification. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
