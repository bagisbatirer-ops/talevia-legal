Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bagis Batirer ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Talevia mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. Talevia does not require account registration and does not collect your email address or personal identity.

1. Who We Are

Talevia is a language learning application developed and operated by Bagis Batirer, an individual developer. If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at support@talevia.app.

2. Information We Collect

Talevia does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the core app.

2.1 Information collected automatically

  • Anonymous identifier: When you first open the app, an anonymous session is created via Firebase Authentication. This identifier has no connection to your real identity.
  • Language preferences: Your native language, the language(s) you are learning, and your proficiency level — stored locally on your device.
  • Learning progress: Your quiz results, reading progress, vocabulary lists, streak data, and badge achievements — stored locally on your device and synced anonymously.
  • Usage data: Anonymous app sessions, features used, and screens visited — used to improve the app.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, app version, and language settings.
  • Crash reports: Anonymous error logs to help us identify and fix technical issues.

2.2 Optional: Connect feature (language exchange)

If you choose to use the Connect feature to practice with other learners, you may optionally provide:

  • Display name: A name or nickname visible to your conversation partners. This does not need to be your real name.

Voice and text data in Connect sessions are transmitted in real time and are not stored or recorded by us.

2.3 Subscription and purchases

If you choose to purchase a Talevia Premium subscription, the transaction is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive only a subscription status confirmation (active/inactive) via RevenueCat — we do not receive your payment details, full name, or email address from these transactions.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share anonymous or minimal data only with trusted service providers who help us operate the App:

  • Firebase (Google): Anonymous authentication and analytics. Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: Backend infrastructure and content delivery. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status management. Privacy Policy
  • AI providers (Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek): Powering vocabulary translations and AI conversation features. These providers process data under their respective privacy policies and do not use your data to train their models.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights. In the event of a business transfer, we will notify users in advance.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Most of your learning data (vocabulary, progress, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Anonymous identifiers and sync data are stored on our servers for as long as your app is active.

If you delete the app, your local data is removed immediately. Anonymous server-side data is deleted within 90 days of inactivity. If you contact us to request deletion of your anonymous data, we will process this within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

Because Talevia does not collect personal identifying information, most data rights apply in a limited way. However, you can:

  • Delete your data: Uninstall the app to delete all local data. Contact us at support@talevia.app to request deletion of any server-side anonymous data.
  • Opt out of analytics: Disable analytics tracking in app settings at any time.
  • Opt out of notifications: Disable notifications in app settings or your device settings.
  • Access your data: Contact us to receive a copy of any data associated with your anonymous identifier.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or California, additional rights may apply. Contact us at support@talevia.app for any requests.

7. Children's Privacy

Talevia is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since we do not collect personal information, the risk is minimal — but if you believe a child is using the Connect feature and has shared personal information, please contact us at support@talevia.app.

8. International Data Transfers

Talevia is a global application. Anonymous data may be processed in countries including the United States and the European Union. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls. Since we do not collect personal identifiers, the impact of any security incident on your privacy is minimal.

10. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party SDKs for analytics and crash reporting. These may collect anonymous device identifiers. You can opt out in the app's settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, by sending an in-app notification.
