Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bağış Batırer ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Talevia mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account data: email address and display name provided during registration (via Firebase Authentication)
  • Usage data: language preferences, learning progress, game scores, reading history
  • Device data: device type, operating system, app version, crash reports
  • Communication data: messages sent through the in-app P2P chat feature (stored temporarily for moderation purposes)
  • Purchase data: subscription status managed via RevenueCat (we do not store payment card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's features
  • To personalize your learning experience
  • To process subscriptions and restore purchases
  • To send push notifications (only if you grant permission)
  • To detect and prevent abuse or violations of our Terms of Service
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at support@talevia.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability (GDPR users)
  • Opt out of sale of personal information (CCPA users — we do not sell personal data)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@talevia.app. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Talevia is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. International Data Transfers

Talevia is a global application. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States and the European Union. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses where required.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The Talevia mobile app does not use browser cookies. We use Firebase Analytics and crash reporting SDKs, which may collect device identifiers for analytics purposes. You can opt out of analytics collection in the App's settings.

10. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any information.

11. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, by sending you an in-app notification. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
