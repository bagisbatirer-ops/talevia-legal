Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Talevia account and all associated data at any time. Please follow the steps below.

⚠️ This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your learning progress, vocabulary, and all personal data cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account

1
Open the Talevia app on your device.
2
Go to Profile → Settings → Account.
3
Tap "Delete Account" and confirm your choice.
4
Your account will be deleted immediately from the app. Server-side data will be fully removed within 30 days.

Can't Access the App?

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email to support@talevia.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your account credentials and profile information
  • Learning progress, vocabulary lists, and game scores
  • Chat history and conversation data
  • Subscription status (cancellation is handled separately via the App Store or Google Play)
  • All anonymous identifiers linked to your account

What Data May Be Retained

  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics data that cannot be linked back to you
  • Records required for legal or fraud prevention purposes (retained for up to 90 days after deletion)

Subscription Cancellation

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. To avoid future charges, please cancel your subscription before requesting account deletion:

  • Google Play: Google Play Store → Subscriptions → Talevia → Cancel
  • App Store: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Talevia → Cancel
